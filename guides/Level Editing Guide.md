Hello, Archie devs, modders, and curious players!
In this guide, I'll explain how to edit, add, and remove the objects in a level!
Levels are broken down into layers. Each layer is a list of objects. Some of the layers(most actual objects, e.g. platforms and hazards, plus scroll trackers), plus the player, are then placed into the first scroll layer. Others(backgrounds) go into the second. Still others are not added to any scroll layer at all, and they are pipelined straight to the ALL_THINGS list.
